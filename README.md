## Harborline Financial Performance Analysis

A management-focused financial performance analysis built by **Jix Analytics & Advisory** using synthetic business data.

## Overview

This project demonstrates how financial and operational reporting can move beyond showing what happened and help management understand **why performance changed and what should be investigated next**.

The analysis examines revenue growth, profitability, margin pressure, operating expenses, budget variance, accounts receivable, and working-capital risk.

## Management Questions

The project focuses on questions such as:

- Is revenue growth translating into stronger profit?
- Where is margin leakage occurring?
- Which expenses are moving away from budget?
- How much cash is tied up in receivables?
- Which performance issues require management attention?

## Key Findings

The analysis identified several areas requiring management attention, including:

- revenue growth that did not translate proportionally into profit growth;
- margin pressure across parts of the business;
- operating-expense and budget-variance concerns;
- significant accounts-receivable exposure;
- approximately **$3.923M in open receivables**, including about **$1.158M past due**.

## Approach

The project combines:

- financial performance analysis;
- KPI and management reporting;
- variance analysis;
- working-capital analysis;
- dashboard-based decision support;
- management-focused interpretation.

The objective is not simply to build charts. It is to connect the numbers to the business questions management needs answered.

## Data Note

Harborline is a fictional company created for portfolio and analytical demonstration purposes. The underlying data is synthetic and does not represent a real client or business.
